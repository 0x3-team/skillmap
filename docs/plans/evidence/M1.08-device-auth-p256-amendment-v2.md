# M1.08 additive amendment — macOS P-256 proof suite V2

Status: **AMENDMENT_CANDIDATE — independent acceptance required**

This is an additive amendment to the accepted M1.08 contract. It does not
edit, replace, or reinterpret `skillmap.ed25519.v1`, its preimages, token
claims, or its non-exportable-key requirement. It defines one macOS-only suite
for the Secure Enclave capability that Apple documents:
`skillmap.ecdsa-p256-sha256.v2`. Until this file and the signed helper boundary are independently
accepted, no M3 product surface may advertise the suite.

## Frozen wire contract

- Capability is the exact closed value `skillmap.ecdsa-p256-sha256.v2`.
- The client must advertise and accept only that exact suite. It must not pick
  an arbitrary server value, omit the suite, fall back to Ed25519, or change
  suite during pairing, token generations, proof nonces, or rotation.
- The public key is exactly 91 bytes of DER SubjectPublicKeyInfo with prefix
  `3059301306072a8648ce3d020106082a8648ce3d030107034200`, followed by
  `04 || X || Y` for an uncompressed P-256 point. The wire form is base64url
  without padding (122 ASCII characters). Thumbprint is lowercase
  `sha256:<hex>` over those exact DER bytes.
- The helper converts Apple's strict, minimally encoded X9.62 DER signature to
  exactly 64 bytes of IEEE P1363 `r || s`, each integer exactly 32-byte
  big-endian and in `[1, n-1]`. DER is never accepted on the wire.
- The V2 proof preimage is UTF-8 for the following 14 lines plus one final LF:

```text
SKILLMAP-DEVICE-PROOF-V2
<proof suite>
<UPPERCASE HTTP METHOD>
<configured HTTPS origin>
<exact normalized origin-relative path>
skillmap.connector.v1
<closed proof purpose>
<22-character device_id>
<device public-key thumbprint>
<body sha256>
<22-character Idempotency-Key or NONE>
<22-character proof nonce>
<issued-at Unix seconds>
<access-token sha256 or NONE>
```

- The V2 idempotency digest is SHA-256 over these nine UTF-8 lines plus a
  final LF:

```text
SKILLMAP-DEVICE-IDEMPOTENCY-V2
<proof suite>
<UPPERCASE HTTP METHOD>
<configured HTTPS origin>
<exact normalized origin-relative path>
skillmap.connector.v1
<closed operation name>
<exact body sha256>
<22-character Idempotency-Key>
```

## Rotation, compatibility, and cutover

The suite is immutable for a device lineage. Same-suite P-256 rotation may
replace the active public key only through the accepted nonce/idempotency and
revision checks. Cross-suite rotation is forbidden; it requires revoke and a
fresh browser pairing. A client receiving `incompatible_client` never retries
with V1. The V1 history remains readable under its original contract.

The suite is feature-disabled until independent acceptance proves Node 22 and
24, the optimized Next server, the emitted OpenNext Worker under Workerd, and
the signed helper on the exact supported macOS configuration. This local
candidate has no such live, emitted-worker, signed, pushed, or deployed proof.

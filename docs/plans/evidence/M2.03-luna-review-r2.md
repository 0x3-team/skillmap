## Review coverage

### Forward-only migration and phase boundary

- The reviewed file is a new sixteenth migration after the accepted fifteen-
  migration baseline; no earlier migration is edited.
- The migration creates only the M2.03 account-owned Managed Skill identity
  and aggregate metadata foundation: role posture, private table, constraints,
  indexes, trigger, forced RLS, and an ungranted owner projection.
- It introduces no lifecycle state, immutable version, release binding, active
  pointer, revision/CAS authority, catalog join/projection change, storage
  authority, runtime behavior, or prompt-routing behavior.
- M2.04 and later remain outside this artifact and approval.

### Least privilege and fail-closed role posture

- `skillmap_vault_definer` is created or validated as `NOLOGIN`, non-superuser,
  non-inheriting, non-replicating, non-bypass-RLS, and without database or role
  creation privileges.
- The role's `search_path` is explicitly empty; the trigger function is
  invoker-mode with an empty search path, avoiding ambient-schema lookup.
- Role membership is checked and application roles cannot assume the future
  definer role.
- Revoke-first posture removes table and view privileges from `PUBLIC`,
  `anon`, `authenticated`, `service_role`, and the future definer role.
- No application-facing direct table or projection grant or service-role
  bypass is introduced.

### Managed Skill identity and ownership

- `private.managed_skills` has exactly the internal UUID identity, generated
  `msk_` public identifier, `auth.users` account ownership, display metadata,
  and server-owned timestamps required by M2.03.
- The public identifier is constrained to exact lowercase
  `^msk_[0-9a-f]{32}$` grammar and is globally unique. Account/internal-ID and
  account/public-ID uniqueness constraints are present.
- The account foreign key uses `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
- Identity, public identity, account ownership, and creation timestamp are
  protected as immutable by the trigger. Display metadata is the mutable
  aggregate surface.
- `display_name` is normalized and bounded to `1..140`; optional description
  is normalized and bounded to `20,000` characters. Control characters and
  over-limit values are rejected, with boundary cases covered in the test.
- Timestamp defaults are server-owned, caller timestamp changes are
  overwritten, and `updated_at >= created_at` is enforced.

### Index, RLS, and projection safety

- Owner access has the required equality-before-range pagination index
  `(account_id, created_at DESC, public_id)` and the account/public-ID unique
  lookup path.
- RLS is enabled and forced on the private table, with zero M2.03 policies.
- The API view is security-invoker and security-barrier, filters by
  `(select auth.uid())`, and exposes exactly five columns:
  `public_id`, `display_name`, `description`, `created_at`, and `updated_at`.
- The view excludes the internal UUID and `account_id`; no enumeration or
  alternate ownership argument is exposed.
- Anonymous and authenticated direct reads fail closed under the reviewed
  privilege posture.
- Existing catalog projections remain separate and unchanged.

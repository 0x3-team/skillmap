1. Use a dedicated Apple-silicon runner and a disposable non-admin local user.
   Do not use the user's ordinary account.

   never by direct PATH lookup: require `/usr/bin/xcrun --find notarytool` to

   `xcrun`/absolute-path check fails or the versions disagree.

   agreements, role, and certificate slots without recording personal account

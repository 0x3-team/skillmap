## Applied schema projection

Read-only catalog inspection against the reset local database produced:

- columns, in order: `id uuid NOT NULL`, `public_id text NOT NULL`, `account_id uuid NOT NULL`, `display_name text NOT NULL`, `description text NULL`, `created_at timestamptz NOT NULL`, `updated_at timestamptz NOT NULL`;
- defaults: core `gen_random_uuid()` for internal/public identity and `statement_timestamp()` for both timestamps;
- eleven constraints: PK, account FK `ON DELETE CASCADE`, global public-ID unique, `(account_id,id)` unique, `(account_id,public_id)` unique, exact `^msk_[0-9a-f]{32}$` grammar, display-name/description length and normalization/control checks, and timestamp ordering;
- five indexes, all constraint- or query-owned: primary key, global public ID, two account composite keys, and `(account_id, created_at DESC, public_id)` owner pagination;
- `relrowsecurity=true`, `relforcerowsecurity=true`, policy count `0`;
- projection options `{security_invoker=true,security_barrier=true}`;
- projection columns exactly `public_id, display_name, description, created_at, updated_at` with an `account_id = (select auth.uid())` predicate; and
- `skillmap_vault_definer` attributes all false for login, superuser, create-database, create-role, inherit, replication, and bypass-RLS.

No direct table/view privilege is granted to `PUBLIC`, `anon`, `authenticated`, `service_role`, or the future definer role in M2.03.
